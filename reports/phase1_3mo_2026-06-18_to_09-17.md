## Takeaways
1. **Jev's momentum reading does not predict the next 30 minutes.** Average forward return is about 0.00% at every P(momentum strong) level, from 0.0–0.2 up to 0.8–1.0. Jev describes the momentum in the numbers we give it; it doesn't forecast. Bad-news probability doesn't predict a drop either.
2. **So no confidence threshold can fix it.** Every one of the 15 settings lost on the unseen days (−2.4% to −15.8%, vs SPY −2.1%). The settings that traded more lost more, which fits random entries paying 0.1% slippage per round trip.
3. **Signal-driven exits are where the money went.** For the chosen setting, exits on Jev's sell signal averaged −0.77% (39 trades): the bot bought momentum and sold into the chop.
4. **Jev cost adds up on $10.** At $0.016/day it's about 5% of the account per month, before any trading losses.
5. **Jev did beat the no-AI rules bot on the tune days** (−6.4% for rules), and it was about even on the test days (−9.4% for rules). Neither has an edge.

**Next idea:** use Jev on text, where a judgment model could have an edge, rather than on numbers we already computed. Trade only when a new headline appears, have Jev judge its likely impact, and measure the returns that follow.
